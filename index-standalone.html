An action's own query

This action already names a query. The form is merged into it: every key a control of the form names is the form's to state, including a key whose boxes are all unchecked; every other key rides through as the action wrote it.

Region

A value composed from several controls

HTML cannot build one value out of an operator and its operand, or a bound and its comparison. A query-param fieldset states the pair as a template; the controls inside it are its variables, and submit nothing of their own. An empty variable withdraws the pair.

Name

No action

With no action the form submits to this page's own address. Its query is the previous submission's, so it is replaced, as a native form replaces it.
